## 1. 已知异策略策略梯度

旧策略 \(\pi_{\theta'}\) 负责采样，新策略 \(\pi_\theta\) 负责学习：

$$
g(\theta)
=
\mathbb{E}_{(s_t,a_t)\sim\pi_{\theta'}}
\left[
\frac{\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)}
{\pi_{\theta'}(a_t\mid s_t)}
A^{\theta'}(s_t,a_t)
\nabla_\theta
\log \pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)
\right]
$$

我们要寻找目标函数 \(J^{\theta'}(\theta)\)，使其满足：

$$
\nabla_\theta J^{\theta'}(\theta)=g(\theta)
$$

## 2. 哪些量与 \(\theta\) 有关

对 \(\theta\) 求梯度时：

- \(\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)\) 与 \(\theta\) 有关；
- \(\pi_{\theta'}(a_t\mid s_t)\) 是冻结的旧策略概率，视为常数；
- \(A^{\theta'}(s_t,a_t)\) 由旧策略数据计算，视为常数；
- 采样分布 \(\pi_{\theta'}\) 不随 \(\theta\) 变化。

因此，只有分子 \(\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)\) 需要求导。

## 3. 定义概率比值

定义：

$$
r_t(\theta)
=
\frac{\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)}
{\pi_{\theta'}(a_t\mid s_t)}
$$

因为分母与 \(\theta\) 无关，所以：

$$
\nabla_\theta r_t(\theta)
=
\frac{1}
{\pi_{\theta'}(a_t\mid s_t)}
\nabla_\theta
\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)
$$

使用对数求导恒等式：

$$
\nabla_\theta \pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)
=
\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)
\nabla_\theta
\log\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)
$$

代入得到：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla_\theta r_t(\theta)
&=
\frac{\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)}
{\pi_{\theta'}(a_t\mid s_t)}
\nabla_\theta
\log\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)
\\
&=
r_t(\theta)
\nabla_\theta
\log\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)
\end{aligned}
$$

这正好对应异策略梯度中的两部分：

$$
\frac{\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)}
{\pi_{\theta'}(a_t\mid s_t)}
\nabla_\theta
\log\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)
$$

## 4. 加入优势函数

由于 \(A^{\theta'}(s_t,a_t)\) 对 \(\theta\) 是常数：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla_\theta
\left[
r_t(\theta)A^{\theta'}(s_t,a_t)
\right]
&=
A^{\theta'}(s_t,a_t)
\nabla_\theta r_t(\theta)
\\
&=
r_t(\theta)
A^{\theta'}(s_t,a_t)
\nabla_\theta
\log\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)
\end{aligned}
$$

再对旧策略采集的数据取期望：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla_\theta
\mathbb{E}_{(s_t,a_t)\sim\pi_{\theta'}}
\left[
r_t(\theta)A^{\theta'}(s_t,a_t)
\right]
=
\mathbb{E}_{(s_t,a_t)\sim\pi_{\theta'}}
\left[
r_t(\theta)
A^{\theta'}(s_t,a_t)
\nabla_\theta
\log\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)
\right]
\end{aligned}
$$

右边正是异策略策略梯度。因此目标函数为：

$$
J^{\theta'}(\theta)
=
\mathbb{E}_{(s_t,a_t)\sim\pi_{\theta'}}
\left[
\frac{\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)}
{\pi_{\theta'}(a_t\mid s_t)}
A^{\theta'}(s_t,a_t)
\right]
$$

## 5. 核心逻辑

整个“反推”只依赖下面这个关系：

$$
\nabla_\theta r_t(\theta)
=
r_t(\theta)
\nabla_\theta
\log\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)
$$

因此，当梯度中看到：

$$
r_t(\theta)
A_t
\nabla_\theta
\log\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)
$$

就可以识别出，它来自下面这个目标项的梯度：

$$
r_t(\theta)A_t
$$

所以异策略策略梯度的目标函数就是：

$$
J^{\theta'}(\theta)
=
\mathbb{E}_{\pi_{\theta'}}
\left[
r_t(\theta)A^{\theta'}(s_t,a_t)
\right]
$$
